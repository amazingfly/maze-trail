# Dinosaur Adventures hero roster

Use the priority column to pick the next model. It estimates how readily a small child could distinguish a type at maze distance from its outline and a simple verbal clue. Favor two large cues visible from different angles, then familiar body plans. The ordering is an art-direction judgment. No children were tested. The separate quality field is filled only after a 3D model has been measured.

Source inventory: `../mazeGen/assets/dinosaurs/hero-roster.json`, cross-checked with the maze pages in `../mazeGen/production/dinosaur-mazes-v2/manifest.json`. All 13 categories occur in both. Each has 16 hero illustrations, four hatching eggs and four baby illustrations. The source's generic “Long-neck dinosaur” is represented by Brachiosaurus in the game. “Raptor” retains the book's generic label.
